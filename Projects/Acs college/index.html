ABOUT US

ACS Medical College and Hospital was started in the year 2007 under the ambit of Dr.MGR Educational and Research Institute. Founder-Chancellor of our Deemed to be University is Dr.A.C.Shanmugam.

ACS Medical College and Hospital offers medical services to majority of urban and rural people. The hospital have state of art facilities to manage wide range of medical and surgical conditions and well equipped laboratories round the clock. Our Hospital provides various state and central government schemes and evening special clinics to provide health care for the people. The institute is involved in community health services [Rural health training centre and urban health training centre] and provides services for needy people.

Our college has well equipped laboratories, skill labs, museum, library, spacious classrooms and auditoriums and transport facilities.Students, apart from academic activities are given opportunities for cultural and sports activities. All religious festivals are celebrated with innovative programs by both faculties and students. Our institute functions to provide medical education to students with opportunities for applied research in order to mould our students as experts in medical profession.
