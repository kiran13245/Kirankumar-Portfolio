About Us

KLR's Taste It is a fast-food restaurant located in Gudiyatham, offering burgers, pizzas, pastas, and Chinese cuisine. You can find it at 15 Gandhi Nagar, Gudiyatham, and on online delivery platforms like Zomato and Swiggy.

Menu Highlights:

  • 🍔 Wide variety: Fast food including pizzas (veg & non-veg), starters, burgers, sandwiches, wraps, rolls, momos, drinks, ice creams & beverages.
  • ⏰ Hours: 10:00 AM to 9:30 PM daily
  • 🌟 Ratings & Atmosphere: Rated ~4.7/5 on Google; homey & neat ambiance.
  • 📍 Location: 15, Gandhi Nagar, Gudiyatham, Vellore, Tamil Nadu
